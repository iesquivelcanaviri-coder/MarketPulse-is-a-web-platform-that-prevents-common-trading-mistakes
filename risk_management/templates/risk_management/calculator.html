What do these inputs mean?

Total hypothetical capital available to the trading account. It is not automatically the amount used to purchase this particular stock.

If capital is $10,000 and the risk budget is 1%, MarketPulse limits the planned trade loss to approximately $100. It does not mean only $100 is invested.

The assumed price of one share when the hypothetical position begins. MarketPulse can populate this using Alpaca's latest available trade price.

A predefined level where the simulation assumes the position is exited when price moves unfavourably. Actual market execution may differ because of gaps, liquidity and slippage.

Optional favourable exit price. It lets MarketPulse compare potential reward with the amount placed at risk.
