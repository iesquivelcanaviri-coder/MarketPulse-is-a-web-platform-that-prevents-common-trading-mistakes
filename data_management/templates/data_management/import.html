{# HTML details/summary provides a native expandable information section. #} How does Market Condition work?
1
Check historical coverage

MarketPulse checks the historical observations available for {{ selected_symbol }}.

2
Refresh from Alpaca if required

Additional daily OHLCV bars can be requested when coverage is insufficient.

3
Analyse returns and volatility

Historical price changes are evaluated to estimate return and volatility behaviour.

4
Compare moving averages

Twenty-period and sixty-period moving averages are used to evaluate historical trend structure.

5
Save classification

The resulting MarketRegime is saved to PostgreSQL.

What do the market conditions mean?
Uptrend

Historical prices show a sustained upward structure.

Downtrend

Historical prices show a sustained downward structure.

Sideways

Strong directional criteria are not satisfied.

High Volatility

Historical returns exceed the volatility threshold used by the classifier.
