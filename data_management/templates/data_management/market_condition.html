How the Analysis Works

How Market Condition Works

MarketPulse takes the historical market observations already stored for the selected asset, measures recent behaviour and converts the statistical result into a simpler historical market classification.

1
Read Stored Market Data

MarketPulse retrieves the selected asset's historical Open, High, Low, Close and Volume observations from PostgreSQL.

2
Examine Price Direction

Recent price behaviour and moving-average information are examined to determine whether prices have generally been rising, falling or showing limited direction.

3
Measure Historical Volatility

The analysis measures how much historical prices varied during the analysed period and considers the strength of the condition.

4
Classify the Market Condition

The internal Market Regime Analysis combines the measurements and stores an easy-to-read historical classification for the user.

What happens in the backend?

The Django market_condition() view receives the selected symbol and checks the stored MarketData records. When sufficient data is available, it calls identify_market_regime() in analysis_tools/analyzers.py . The calculated result is stored as a MarketRegime record in PostgreSQL. Django then reloads this page and supplies the most recent result through latest_regime .
