About MarketPulse

MarketPulse is an educational trading decision-support platform that combines market research, strategy testing, risk management and user collaboration. Its purpose is to encourage a more structured approach to evaluating trading decisions, rather than relying only on price movements or historical backtest performance.

Main Purpose Research • Test • Analyse • Manage Risk • Communicate

Educational Notice: MarketPulse is designed for educational and decision-support purposes. Market information, backtesting results, strategy analysis, risk calculations and user-generated content do not constitute personalised financial, investment or trading advice. Users remain responsible for conducting their own research and evaluating risk before making financial decisions.
