About MarketPulse

MarketPulse is an educational trading decision-support platform that combines market research, strategy testing, risk management, monitoring and user communication. Its purpose is to encourage a more structured approach to trading decisions by bringing market information, analytical tools and risk controls together in one application.

MarketPulse Workflow Research → Test → Analyse → Manage Risk → Monitor → Communicate
Trading & Analysis
Data

Explore Alpaca assets, historical OHLCV prices, charts and changing market conditions.

Strategies

Build, backtest and compare trading strategies while checking robustness and potential overfitting.

Risk

Calculate position size, examine stop-loss levels and evaluate reward-to-risk before entering a trade.

Dashboard

Brings strategies, backtests, market information and alerts together in one monitoring workspace.
